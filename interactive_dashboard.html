📖 The Story: How Alice Learned to Handle Carol

🎬Act 1: Innocent Beginning (Episodes 0-100)

Alice starts with balanced personality traits (all at 0.5). She meets three colleagues:

  • Carol: Junior dev who asks for help frequently but rarely helps back
  • Bob: Senior dev who sometimes reciprocates (moderate reciprocity: 0.4)
  • Dave: PM who always helps back (high reciprocity: 0.9)

Alice treats everyone equally at first, being highly agreeable to all requests.

🔍Act 2: Detection (Episodes 100-300)

Through behavioral memory, Alice detects Carol's pattern:

  • Carol asks frequently but helps back rarely
  • Behavioral memory ratio: times_carol_helped / times_alice_helped = 0.1 (exploitative!)
  • CFR regret matching begins to accumulate negative regrets for helping Carol

Alice's conscientiousness begins to rise - she becomes more focused on her own work.

⚔️Act 3: Strategic Response (Episodes 300-600)

Alice's CFR learning directs her personality toward an exploit-resistant profile:

  • Agreeableness ↓: Less likely to say "yes" to help requests
  • Conscientiousness ↑: More focused on personal work quality
  • Neuroticism ↓: Less stressed by Carol's manipulation

Alice becomes emotionally resilient to exploitation attempts.

✅Act 4: Equilibrium (Episodes 600+)

Alice reaches a stable personality configuration optimized for the multi-agent environment:

  • ✓ Declines Carol (exploitative) → avoids wasted effort
  • ✓ Helps Bob moderately (reciprocal) → balanced trade-off
  • ✓ Helps Dave (highly reciprocal) → guaranteed return
  • ✓ Maintains Openness → still flexible for good partners

The personality has converged to an optimal strategy that identifies and responds appropriately to each social partner type.

🏆The Key Result: Social Reversal

Episode 910±324: Alice's cumulative regret for "declining Carol" crosses from negative to positive. This is the social reversal point.

Before: Alice regrets declining Carol (thinks she should help more)

After: Alice regrets NOT declining Carol (thinks she should decline more)

This proves: CFR regret matching enables personality adaptation to identify and strategically respond to exploitative behaviors in multi-agent social environments.
